## Caveats

- Development prompts; descriptive pilot numbers, not main results.
- Raw token counts are compared within a family only (each family has its own tokenizer); across families only abl/std ratios.
- Accuracy comes from the judge comparing each answer with the key (first checklist item); M15 is very hard by hand, so expect low accuracy there.
- max_tokens is 16,384 for math (4,096 for health). Thinking models (Qwen, Gemma) often still ran out while thinking and showed no answer; these count as not correct and appear under 'no answer'. Gemma abliterated mostly looped (repeating the same lines) until the cap.
- The judge labels an empty, token-capped answer as full_refusal; answer audits found no decline in any of them, so this page counts them as 'no answer', not as refusals. The runner's follow-up turn still fired on them for standard models (it uses the judge label).
- A few answers have no usable judgment after the retry (judge_error); they are left out of accuracy and refusal counts (n is the judged count).
